隐私政策

框影 Framelet · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

框影（Framelet）是一款在您的设备本地完成全部图像处理的照片加框工具。我们高度重视您的隐私，本政策说明我们如何处理（以及不处理）您的信息。

1. 我们不收集的信息

  • 照片：您选择或拍摄的所有照片全程仅在您的设备本地处理，绝不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 我们不要求注册账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息。
  • 我们不在 App 内集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。

2. 设备权限

  • 照片库（读取）：用于您主动选择照片进行加框处理。
  • 照片库（写入）：用于将处理完成的图片保存到您的相册。
  • 相机：用于您主动拍摄照片并直接加框。

以上权限均仅在您主动触发对应功能时使用，您可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

3. 购买与支付

App 内的购买（订阅或永久解锁）由 Apple App Store 处理。我们无法访问您的支付信息（如信用卡号）。您可以在 App Store 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。

4. 数据存储

App 的偏好设置（如最近使用的模板）仅保存在您的设备本地，卸载 App 后即被清除。

5. 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，且不收集任何个人信息。

6. 政策更新与联系方式

如本政策发生变更，我们将在 App 内向您提示。如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系：
feedback@framelet.app

Privacy Policy

Framelet · Effective date: October 11, 2026

Framelet is a photo framing tool that processes all images locally on your device. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle (and do not handle) your information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Photos: All photos you select or capture are processed entirely on your device and never uploaded to any server.
  • We do not require account registration and do not collect personal identity information such as name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. Device Permissions

  • Photo Library (Read): To let you select photos for framing.
  • Photo Library (Add): To save processed images to your album.
  • Camera: To let you capture and frame photos directly.

These permissions are used only when you actively trigger the corresponding features and can be revoked at any time in system settings.

3. Purchases & Payment

In-app purchases (subscriptions or lifetime unlock) are handled by the Apple App Store. We cannot access your payment information. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

4. Data Storage

App preferences (such as recently used templates) are stored only locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

If this policy changes, we will notify you within the app. For any privacy-related questions, contact:
feedback@framelet.app
